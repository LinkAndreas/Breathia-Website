2. No account, no server, no tracking

  • Breathia does not require or offer an account and never asks for your name, email address or other contact details.
  • The app does not send any data to servers operated by us or by third parties.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or profiling tools and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

3. Data processed on your devices

To provide its features, Breathia processes the following information locally on your devices:

  • Session history: the technique, date, time and duration of each breathing session, and — if a sensor was connected — its heart-rate and HRV readings and trends.
  • Live sensor data: heart rate and the time between heartbeats (RR intervals) received from your Bluetooth heart-rate sensor or, on Apple Watch, from a workout session. Breathia uses them to calculate HRV (RMSSD or SDNN) and show live feedback.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as the selected technique, cues, appearance and HRV metric.

This information never leaves your devices except as described in sections 5 and 6 (Apple Health, and syncing between your Apple Watch and iPhone) and is not accessible to us. You can delete individual sessions in History at any time; deleting the app removes all of its data from that device.

Heart-rate and HRV readings are health data. Because they are processed exclusively on your devices, under your control, and are never transmitted to us, we do not process them within the meaning of the GDPR.

4. Bluetooth

Breathia uses Bluetooth solely to connect to your heart-rate sensor (for example a Polar H10) via the standard Bluetooth Heart Rate service. The app does not scan for, store or collect information about any other devices. Bluetooth access requires your permission, which you can revoke at any time in your device’s settings.

5. Apple Health

Integration with Apple Health is optional and requires your explicit permission.

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: if you turn it on, Breathia saves each completed session to Apple Health as mindful minutes. It only writes this data — it never reads your health data.
  • Apple Watch: during a session, Breathia reads your heart rate through a workout session to show live feedback, and saves the session to Apple Health as a Mind & Body workout and as mindful minutes.

Data in Apple Health is managed by Apple on your devices and is subject to Apple’s privacy policy. You can change or revoke Breathia’s access at any time in the Health app. Breathia never passes health data to us or to third parties.

6. Syncing between Apple Watch and iPhone

Sessions you complete on Apple Watch are transferred to your paired iPhone using Apple’s Watch Connectivity framework so they appear in History. The transfer takes place directly between your devices; the data does not pass through our servers.

7. Feedback by email

If you choose to send feedback from within the app, your email app opens with a message addressed to us. It contains the app version, device model, operating-system version and language, so we can reproduce problems; you can review and edit the message before sending it. If you send it, we process your email address, your message and these details solely to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed.

8. Ratings and diagnostics provided by Apple

Breathia may occasionally ask you to rate the app using Apple’s standard rating prompt; Apple processes any rating you submit. If you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with anonymized crash reports and usage statistics. We cannot identify you from this information.

When you open the website, your browser necessarily transmits technical information, such as your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page or file requested, the referring page and your browser type and version. Cloudflare’s Email Obfuscation is active for the linkandreas.de zone, which this website belongs to: it rewrites the email links on these pages, and a small Cloudflare script decodes them again in your browser. This collects no personal data beyond what is described above.

11. Your rights

As we hold no personal data from the app — except, if you write to us, your email — most of these rights are fulfilled by the app’s design: your data stays on your devices, where you can view and delete it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
